About the lab

Ergonomics for people and the planet

The Green Ergonomic Lab is led by Dr. Yogi Tri Prasetyo. Our work connects green ergonomics and sustainable manufacturing with macroergonomics and job design, consumer behavior, human-computer interaction, Kansei engineering and strategic product design, human behavior in disaster risk reduction, and accident analysis and prevention.
